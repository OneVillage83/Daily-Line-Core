## Why staging used DDC

DDC was already the certified cross-sport evidence boundary and provided a durable existing repository while the dedicated DLC repository had not yet been created. Staging architecture in DDC did **not** make DLC a DDC submodule and did not change DDC's certified authority boundaries.

## Extraction decision

The owner created `OneVillage83/Daily-Line-Core` on 2026-09-17 and authorized seeding it as the canonical home.

The new repository therefore becomes the canonical logical home for:

- cross-sport `SportDecisionPackage` admission;
- cross-sport All Bets assembly;
- EdgeStack optimization;
- cross-sport product ranking/indexing;
- final `DailyLinePublicationPackage` sealing;
- downstream product consumer contracts.

## Authority preserved during extraction

- DDC remains provider/evidence/market-data infrastructure.
- Daily-Model-Core remains independent modeling infrastructure through the calibrated fair-view boundary.
- sport repos retain sport-native models, fair probabilities, individual-market value/Recommendation Gates, joint sport evidence and settlement.
- DLC owns only the cross-sport product layer built from those sealed authorities.
- TDLA remains downstream automation/content/marketing infrastructure and a Bridge proving ground.

## Extraction status

Canonical architecture and roadmap files now live in this repository.

The DDC staging directory should be retained/demoted as a historical pointer rather than treated as current architecture authority. DDC issue #5 remains open until all extraction acceptance items are complete, including deliberate Bridge catalog registration after TDLA proving-ground acceptance.
